Questions (always the same)
  1. How were you before we started (body, energy, work)?
  2. What had you tried and why didn't it work?
  3. What helped most?
  4. What changed at work and at home, beyond the body?
  5. Who would you recommend it to?
Rules
  • Nothing invented or "rounded up". No data, no number.
  • Written permission saved (screenshot).
  • Lab values: anonymised, never with a name or face.
  • No face? Crop from the neck down or blur.
  • In ads, never before/after (Meta rules).
