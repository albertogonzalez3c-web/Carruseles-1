Your niche in one sentence · Your method in 30 seconds

I help busy professionals and business owners over 30 settle the one debt they keep postponing — their body and their energy — with a plan read from their own bloodwork that fits a full calendar.

BodyLAB Reset · hormonal-metabolic reset (PL: reset hormonalno-metaboliczny): we read your labs and body composition first, then fix leptin, cortisol, testosterone, estradiol and insulin with food, training, sleep and — when the labs call for it — targeted support. Pillar 1 · Read

Bloodwork + body composition before any plan. Testosterone, SHBG, estradiol, cortisol, insulin/HOMA-IR, leptin, thyroid. Your numbers, not the mirror, decide.
Why: as a bodybuilder I pushed my body with extremes — olive oil by the glass, doping as a tool. I learned the hard way what hormones do when you ignore them. Today I read the labs before I touch anything.

Pillar 2 · Fit

The plan fits your calendar: 3 sessions of 45–60 min, normal food (steak, pizza, business lunch), travel and late meetings included.
Why: I ate 5–6 forced meals a day "until I threw up". It worked for the stage and wrecked my life. Now I run a coaching business and a supplement store — my own plan has to fit a full day too.

Pillar 3 · System

A system that works on the days motivation doesn't: weekly check-in, sleep window, a default week you can run on autopilot.
Why: for years I said "tomorrow for sure" and didn't go. At the end of 2022 a lot fell apart at once. I didn't come back with motivation — I came back with routine: sleep, food, training, step by step.
