Channel 2 · 100 conversations a day · 2 rounds of 50

Where busy people 30+ already are

Not spam: finding your client where he already is and talking about something of his. Never sell in the first message, never a link in the first message.

Where to find them
  1. Around your own profile: new followers, story viewers, people who like or comment your posts. Always first.
  2. Polish business accounts: entrepreneur podcasts, business media, local business groups, coworkings in your city — the people who comment there.
  3. Store customers who followed @kopalnia.supli_.
  4. Gyms and clubs in your city: members who post training before or after work.
  5. Location tags of business districts, conferences and events.
  6. Hashtags: #przedsiębiorca #biznes #ceo #właścicielfirmy #pracazdalna + city.
Criteria · all of them
  • Roughly 30–55.
  • Works with responsibility or owns a business (bio, posts, LinkedIn link, office stories).
  • Lives in Poland, writes in Polish.
  • Active in the last 7 days, public profile.
  • Not a coach and not selling plans.
How not to burn the account
  • Before writing: follow, like 2 posts and reply to a story. The DM lands as a conversation, not in requests.
  • Each round of 50 in blocks of 10–15 with breaks, never 50 in a row.
  • Rotate the 7 openers and personalise the first sentence.
  • Week 1: 2 × 25. Week 2: 2 × 40. Week 3: 2 × 50.

Poles are dry · don't take it personally

Short answers are normal, not a no

Polish communication is direct and economical, especially in writing and especially with men 30+ in business. What feels cold is usually just efficient. Here's how to read it and when to push.

Style rules for your messages
  • Short and direct: 1–3 sentences. Say the time, the price, the next step. Directness is respect.
  • Little enthusiasm: no "Super!!! 🔥🔥". One emoji at most, often none. American-style hype reads as fake.
  • Don't apologise for writing ("przepraszam, że przeszkadzam"). It lowers your position.
  • Ty by default on Instagram, with a capital letter (Ty, Ciebie, Twój) — polite and modern. First WhatsApp to someone 45+ from the website: "Dzień dobry" and mirror their tone.
  • Facts beat promises: "500+ metamorfoz", "badania", "3 treningi w tygodniu". Poles distrust big claims.
  • When to insist: no answer → days 2, 7, 14, then stop. After a call: 3 touches in 5 days. A soft "maybe" always gets a concrete date.

Channel 3 · Follow Me Ads · voice-over

300 € a month, only to get followers

The ad doesn't sell. It brings busy Poles 30+ to your profile, they see the proof, and they enter channels 2 and 4 (you welcome every new follower by hand).

The piece
  • 30–45 s Reel, your voice-over over clips of you working, in the store, training, cooking.
  • One angle per ad, hook in the first 3 s (3 versions).
  • Close: "Obserwuj…". Subtitles always.
  • Promote the organic voice-over that performed best.
Setup
  • From the Reel: Promote → More profile visits.
  • ≈ 10 €/day (≈ 300 €/month), one ad at a time at the start. Evaluate after 2 days.
  • Audience: Poland, 30+ (30–55), language Polish, interests in business / entrepreneurship / fitness — or Advantage+ with Polish language.
Traffic light (€ per follower)
  • Under 1 € (≈ 4,30 zł): keep it and scale.
  • 1–1,5 € (≈ 4,30–6,50 zł): change the hook, same body.
  • Over 1,5 € after 2 days: cut it.
  • Log every ad in your weekly report.
Meta rules

Never tell the viewer what's happening to his body ("Masz brzuch?", "Brak energii?", "Niski testosteron?" are banned). First person ("I used to crash at 4 pm") or "most people" ("większość"). No before/after, no "X kg in Y weeks". No peptides, TRT or hormone talk in Meta ads: Meta rejects them and can block the ad account — keep those topics for organic content.
