My story

I used to weigh 216 lbs. I know what it's like not to feel good in your body.

As a kid I bought my clothes in the plus-size section and suffered because I didn't feel physically capable. For years I did what most people do: more cardio, fewer calories and diets I quit after two weeks. It didn't work.

I studied Physiotherapy and started Medicine. I left it to do this. And I understood what nobody had explained to me: if the engine isn't tuned on the inside, the vehicle won't perform. When I fixed my sleep, my stress and my digestion, my body started to respond.

I've been training naturally for 10 years and I've competed 11 times, placing first and second in natural categories. I've also been through food anxiety and periods of stress and change. I know what it feels like, so I won't judge you.

In 6+ years of online coaching I've worked with more than 1,800 people. What I've learned is simple: everything starts by repairing the inside. That's health. The physique comes after.

The method

From the inside out.

Most plans start with calories and exercises. I start with what makes your body respond to them.

FAQ

Quick answers

How much time do I need each day?

The plan fits your schedule. Many of the people I work with have long workdays and families. The key is doing what has the most impact, not doing more.

Do I have to eat weird food or weigh everything?

No. We start from what you like and how you eat now, and adjust from there. Eating out or traveling isn't a problem: message me and we sort it out.

What does the support look like?

You have WhatsApp with me for day-to-day questions and a weekly check-in where we review how you've been and adjust the plan.

Do I talk to you or to a team?

To me. I handle the plan, the WhatsApp and the check-ins myself.

What if I have a medical condition or take medication?

Tell me at the start. This coaching doesn't replace your doctor's diagnosis or treatment: we work on your habits alongside it, and if something needs a medical assessment, I'll tell you.

What if I don't see results?

If you do your part and there are no results, or if I don't support you the way I promise, I refund your investment. It's in the contract.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your case. Message me and I'll tell you once I know what you need.
